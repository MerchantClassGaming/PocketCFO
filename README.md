# PocketCFO

Know what your crafting is actually worth. PocketCFO shows you which orders and recipes make gold, and which ones don't, right inside the profession window.

## What it does

- **Crafting orders at a glance.** See the profit and Knowledge Point rewards for every order on the board without clicking into each one.
- **Make it or buy it?** Every recipe shows what it costs you to craft, priced from real Auction House value instead of the single cheapest listing.
- **Processing.** See whether milling, prospecting, recycling and similar conversions are worth your time.
- **Resourcefulness and Multicraft built in.** The numbers include the extra materials and items those stats return.
- **Gold tracker.** Your gold per character and across your account.

## Beta features

Inventory tracking and Auction House posting tools are included as beta features. Turn them on in settings (type **/pcfo**) if you want to try them early.

## Getting started

1. Open any profession window or the crafting orders board.
2. Type **/pcfo** to open settings.
