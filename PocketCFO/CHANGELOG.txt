Forever and Retail
Valuation now falls back to last scanned if Blizzard doesn't report a realm on login. This addresses a recent update from Blizzard (bug or "feature") where realm names are not always being sent.

Shopping lists
Add items to a shopping list from the recipe analysis window. This will look at reagents you already have in inventory (bags or bank) and add how many more you need to make the desired quantity of outputs. Open the shopping list at any time by shift+clicking on the Pocket CFO minimap icon. Add things manually to the list when the list is open.

Fixed 2 bugs causing the gold tracker to double count certain drops in dungeons.

AH Smart Pricing
Made the notes a little easier to understand what was really happening.

Item Tooltips
Now shows where and how many of an item you have.

Gold Tracker
Automatic gathering sessions now include gold earned from mob loot and chests collected during the run.

Forever Only
Merchant Favor analysis
On the Pocket CFO Dashboard, you now have an "analysis" section. The first item added here is the crate analysis. Pushing the crate analysis button will run through all of the waylaid crates available and the cheapest way to fill them from current prices, giving you a "cost per favor" calculation and showing you the cheapest way to gain favor. Clicking the "add" button on the right side of the analysis will add missing items to your shopping list (1 add button for each line in the analysis).

Gold Tracker no longer resets if you die in a dungeon and run back/reenter to resurrect.

Retail Only
Patron Orders
Orders that will award 1 knowledge point for a first craft bonus now show the bonus in the order summary as +1KP (added to any rewards that also give knowledge points), meaning you can see +1, +2, or +3 KP depending on first craft bonus and rewards.

Shopping lists
On retail, you get buttons on the orders screens to add missing items to the shopping list to complete profitable orders or all orders.
