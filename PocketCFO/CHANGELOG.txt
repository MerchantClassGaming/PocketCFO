Fixed a bug where sometimes an item sold by vendors would overstate its valuation if a vendor sold it for more than current AH listings.

Custom gold tracking sessions keep running through a logout or a reload.

You will get a “Still gathering?” prompt after 3 minutes with no gather on an automatic gathering run.
