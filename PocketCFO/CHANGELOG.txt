Scans by realm is now supported. This means that if you play on multiple realms, you'll need to do a new scan for each unconnected realm. Connected realms will share scan data.

Scan optimization - phase 1 would sometimes cut short on larger servers causing the scan to hang. Phase 3 also had an issue with larger servers where unpriced items would leave the scan hung and blizzard never sends updated information that would allow the scan to move forward with the data it was able to obtain.

A scan canceled by closing the Auction House now still starts the 15-minute cooldown, because Blizzard’s own cooldown already counts it. 

Improved the way the patron orders board evaluates recipes. The board now evaluates both low and high quality mat completions of the recipe when an order requires higher quality outputs. It will tell you the cheapest way to fulfill the order, low quality mats and concentration or high quality mats, and present you with that option on the order board summary. It will also mark a recipe with a quality requirement you can't meet with mats and concentration to red and move it to the bottom with any orders you can't complete because you don't have the recipe.

Added a profit/loss view on each recipe in the bottom left that can be seen without having to open the recipe or crafting analysis window.

Improved valuation on darkmoon decks. Lower level decks will now not exceed the value of higher level decks.

Improved valuation of profession equipment - T5 items will always hold the highest value. Anything of lesser quality will cap at 10% less per tier from the T5, so a T4 will cap at 90%, T3 will cap at 80%, etc. Blues also cap greens of the same tool type. For example, the T1 blue enchanting rod (ilvl 206) will always value higher than the green T5 enchanting rod.

Sorted out an issue with mailbox timing and the gold tracker

Fixed an issue that caused resourcefulness to sometimes not calculate on the patron order board

Resolved some of the "unknown" gold sources so they track properly to their corresponding buckets
