v1.1.0
New: See where your gold comes from
Open it with /pcfo gold. The Gold window now has three tabs:

Balances
Your gold on every character, your Warband Bank, and your account total. Same as before.

Income
Where your gold came from: Patron Orders, Personal Orders, Auction House, Vendor, Loot & Quests, and more.

Pick Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, or All, and pick one character or all of them.
Click a source to see it split by profession, or to see which items you sold on the Auction House or to vendors.
A bar for each day shows how your income changes over time. Hover a bar to see that day.
Auction House sales are counted on the day they sold, not the day you collected the mail. Open your mailbox often to keep it up to date.
Gold you move between your own characters is never counted as income.
Orders
What your crafting orders actually earned you: the gold paid, plus the value of the rewards and any mats you got back, minus the mats you used.

See it by profession, then click a profession to compare Patron, Personal, Public, and Guild orders.
Uncheck order types to hide them. For example, uncheck everything but Patron to see which profession's patron orders pay best.
Tracking starts when you install this update, so your history builds up from today.
