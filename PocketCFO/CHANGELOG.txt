Merchant Favor analysis
- Use this to figure out the cheapest way to earn a specific amount of merchant's favor based on current auction house pricing.
- Analysis now has its own tab along the bottom of the Pocket CFO Auction House window.
- Type in how much Favor you want before you run it. Analyze scans the Auction House (about a minute and a half), or uses your last scan if it's less than 15 minutes old, so the answer comes from current prices. The scan also refreshes all your item values.
- The plan shows the cheapest way to reach that Favor. Each line is a crate, what to fill it with, how many, the gold and the gold per Favor. As the cheap listings run out, the plan moves to the next cheapest option, so the same crate can show up twice with two different fills.
- Everything is priced at what it sells for, even items you already own, since you could sell them instead.
- Shows when a higher level would make it cheaper (for example "At level 20 this would cost 2g less").
- One "Add plan to shopping list" button adds the whole plan. Your Shopping List takes off what you already have.
- Runs at your own faction's Auction House only.

Separate prices for each Auction House
- Alliance, Horde and Neutral Auction Houses now keep their own prices, separately for each ruleset (Normal, PvP and so on). A scan at Booty Bay no longer mixes into your faction's prices.
- Everything in PocketCFO uses your faction's prices. If you've scanned a Neutral Auction House, item tooltips also show a "Neutral AH" line with that price and how long ago it was scanned, so you can see which side pays more.
